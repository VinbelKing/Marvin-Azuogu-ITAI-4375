## Course Description

Exploration of artificial intelligence applications in healthcare, covering data analysis, clinical decision support, diagnostic AI, natural language processing, reinforcement learning, federated learning, and ethical frameworks for responsible AI deployment.

## Key Themes

- **Diagnostic AI:** Sensitivity/specificity tradeoffs, liquid biopsy, retinopathy screening
- **Prognostic modeling:** Multimodal ML fusion, AUC-ROC, SHAP explainability
- **Privacy-preserving AI:** Federated learning, differential privacy, HIPAA compliance
- **Ethics & equity:** Algorithmic bias, SDOH, transparency, accountability
- **Clinical workflow integration:** CDSS, NLP in EHRs, conversational AI, stakeholder engagement
- **Autonomous systems:** Reinforcement learning, closed-loop drug delivery
