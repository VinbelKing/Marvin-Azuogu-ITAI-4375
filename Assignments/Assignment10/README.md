# Assignment 10 — Stakeholder Engagement Plan for AI in Healthcare

**Course:** ITAI 4375 — AI in Healthcare  
**Institution:** Houston City College  
**Instructor:** Dr. Doreen Rosenstrauch

## Overview

Comprehensive stakeholder engagement plan for implementing an AI-powered diagnostic system in a clinical setting. Identifies four key stakeholder groups, their concerns, and tailored engagement strategies.

## Stakeholder Groups

### 1. Physicians
- **Primary Concerns:** Clinical accuracy, liability if AI recommendation is wrong, workflow disruption, loss of professional autonomy
- **Engagement Strategy:** Involve physicians in system validation, provide peer-reviewed evidence, position AI as decision support (not replacement)
- **Key Message:** "AI augments your expertise — you remain the decision-maker"

### 2. Nurses
- **Primary Concerns:** Increased documentation burden, alert fatigue, unclear responsibility boundaries
- **Engagement Strategy:** Workflow integration testing with nurse input, training on AI output interpretation, feedback channels
- **Key Message:** "Your bedside observations remain irreplaceable context the AI cannot replicate"

### 3. Patients
- **Primary Concerns:** Privacy and data security, understanding how AI influences their care, trust in AI-driven decisions
- **Engagement Strategy:** Plain-language explanation of AI role in care, opt-in consent processes, patient-facing transparency
- **Key Message:** "Your doctor still makes all final decisions — AI helps them have more complete information"

### 4. Administrators
- **Primary Concerns:** ROI and cost justification, regulatory compliance, liability exposure, staff training costs
- **Engagement Strategy:** Present business case with outcome metrics, compliance documentation, phased rollout plan
- **Key Message:** "Early adopter position reduces long-term risk and improves competitive standing"

## Feedback and Resistance Strategies

- Establish ongoing feedback loops post-deployment (surveys, incident reporting)
- Create a multidisciplinary AI governance committee
- Address resistance through transparent communication about limitations and error rates
- Designate physician and nurse AI champions for peer-to-peer trust-building

## Concepts Covered

- Change management for healthcare technology implementation
- Stakeholder analysis and mapping
- Communication strategy tailored by audience
- AI governance and oversight structures
- Managing resistance to clinical AI adoption
