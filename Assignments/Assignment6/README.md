# Assignment 6 — Social Determinants of Health (SDOH) and AI

**Course:** ITAI 4375 — AI in Healthcare  
**Institution:** Houston City College  
**Instructor:** Dr. Doreen Rosenstrauch

## Overview

Exploration of how Social Determinants of Health (SDOH) can be integrated into AI-driven healthcare systems to improve patient outcomes and reduce health disparities, along with the ethical concerns this integration raises.

## Key SDOH Domains Examined

- **Economic Stability:** Income, employment, food security, housing stability — directly affects ability to access and adhere to care
- **Neighborhood and Built Environment:** Access to healthy food, safe housing, transportation, environmental exposures
- **Education Access and Quality:** Health literacy, ability to navigate the healthcare system, preventive health behaviors

## How SDOH Integration Improves Outcomes

- AI models incorporating SDOH data can better risk-stratify patients beyond clinical variables alone
- Enables targeted interventions for high-risk social situations before acute events occur
- Supports resource allocation to underserved populations
- Improves care coordination by surfacing non-clinical barriers

## Ethical Concerns

- **Privacy:** Collection and use of sensitive socioeconomic data raises consent and surveillance concerns
- **Algorithmic Bias:** If historical SDOH data reflects systemic inequities, models may perpetuate or amplify discrimination
- **Medicalization of Social Problems:** Risk of pathologizing poverty or social circumstances rather than addressing root causes
- **Data Accuracy:** Self-reported or inferred SDOH data may be incomplete or unreliable

## Concepts Covered

- Social Determinants of Health framework (Healthy People 2030)
- SDOH-informed AI risk stratification
- Health equity and disparities in AI system design
- Ethical principles: fairness, privacy, beneficence
- Algorithmic bias in socially-informed models
