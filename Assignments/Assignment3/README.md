# Assignment 3 — Future of AI-Powered Clinical Decision Support Systems

**Course:** ITAI 4375 — AI in Healthcare  
**Institution:** Houston City College  
**Instructor:** Dr. Doreen Rosenstrauch

## Overview

Forward-looking scenario analysis of AI-powered Clinical Decision Support Systems (CDSS) projected to 2030–2035. Uses Glass Health as the current baseline and envisions transformative capabilities in multimodal AI, ambient intelligence, and federated learning.

## Baseline: Glass Health (Current State)

Glass Health is a contemporary AI-powered CDSS that assists clinicians with differential diagnosis and clinical reasoning. It serves as the starting point for projecting future CDSS evolution.

## 2030–2035 Projected Capabilities

- **Multimodal Intelligence:** Integration of imaging, genomic, lab, and clinical note data into unified diagnostic models
- **Ambient AI:** Passive monitoring of clinical encounters to surface real-time decision support without disrupting workflow
- **Federated Learning:** Cross-institutional model training while preserving patient data privacy
- **Predictive Analytics:** Shift from reactive diagnosis to proactive risk identification and prevention

## Ethical Challenges

- Algorithmic bias and health equity concerns
- Clinician over-reliance and deskilling
- Transparency and explainability requirements
- Patient consent and data governance in federated systems

## Concepts Covered

- Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) architecture and evolution
- Multimodal AI in healthcare
- Federated learning for privacy-preserving model development
- Ambient clinical intelligence
- Ethical frameworks for AI deployment in clinical settings
