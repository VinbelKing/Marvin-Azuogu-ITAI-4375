# Assignment 7 — Leveraging AI Technology for Improved Healthcare Outcomes

**Course:** ITAI 4375 — AI in Healthcare  
**Institution:** Houston City College  
**Instructor:** Dr. Doreen Rosenstrauch

## Overview

Case study analysis of AI-assisted diabetic retinopathy screening in a primary care setting. Examines how the EyeArt AI system identified vision-threatening disease in an asymptomatic patient and explores risks and mitigation strategies.

## Case Study: Mrs. Santos

- **Patient:** 58-year-old asymptomatic woman with Type 2 diabetes
- **Setting:** Routine primary care visit
- **AI Tool:** EyeArt autonomous diabetic retinopathy screening system
- **Finding:** Bilateral diabetic retinopathy detected by AI screening

Without AI screening, this patient's condition would likely have gone undetected until symptomatic — by which point significant, irreversible vision loss may have already occurred.

## AI Risks Identified

1. **False Positive Risk:** AI may flag non-pathological findings, causing unnecessary specialist referrals and patient anxiety
2. **Alert Fatigue:** High volume of AI-generated findings may cause clinicians to discount or overlook genuine alerts
3. **Health Equity Gaps:** AI systems trained on non-diverse datasets may underperform for certain demographic groups

## How AI Was Leveraged

- **Early Detection:** Identified pre-symptomatic retinopathy enabling timely intervention
- **Equity:** Brought specialist-level screening capability to a primary care setting, expanding access
- **Risk Stratification:** Enabled prioritization of ophthalmology referrals based on disease severity

## Concepts Covered

- Autonomous AI in diagnostic screening workflows
- Diabetic retinopathy as an AI use case (well-defined, high-stakes, image-based)
- Risk identification and mitigation in clinical AI deployment
- Health equity considerations in AI screening programs
- EyeArt and FDA-cleared autonomous diagnostic AI
