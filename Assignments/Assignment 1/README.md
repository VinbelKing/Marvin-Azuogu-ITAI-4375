# Medical Terminology Identification Assignment

## Overview
This assignment requires students to identify and analyze at least 10 medical terms from 3-5 healthcare sources, providing definitions, contextual usage, and clinical significance for each term.

## Requirements
- **Source Material:** 3-5 recent medical journal articles or healthcare news reports
- **Medical Terms:** Minimum 10 terms identified
- **Format:** Word document (.docx) or PDF
- **Formatting:** 12pt Times New Roman/Arial, double-spaced, 1-inch margins
- **Length:** Minimum 200 words (main content)
- **Citations:** APA format (in-text citations + reference list)

## Submission
Upload completed assignment to the designated course platform submission area.
